Scope: the whole Omnific web app (apps/web): home, job page, explore, documents, demos. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience: learners, educators, engineers and creators equally (PRODUCT.md). Task: describe a scenario, follow the
build, explore and judge the result, accept or ask for changes, publish.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Motion frozen and checked. Omnific is the studio where any physical event is photographed as a strobe study:
the result is shown as its own multiple exposure. Refuses the category default of a white page, centred prompt box,
indigo accent and three equal demo cards.

OWN-WORLD: Black studio ground (#0B0C10) with lifted graphite surfaces, flash-white type (#F2F2EE), and three gel
flashes doing real jobs: magenta #FF3D7F (action, current), cyan #3DE0FF (data and links), yellow #FFD23D (primary
action, success flash). Archivo for display and UI at tight tracking, JetBrains Mono only for numbers and
measurements. Hairline studio seams, no glass. Light theme is the contact-sheet print: paper white ground, ink type,
the same gels deepened for contrast.

STORY: The visitor sees immediately that plain words become a moving, checked physical event; watches the build
fire one flash per stage; then plays, strobes, drags and resets the result, reads why to trust it, and publishes it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: header with the wordmark left and Explore / Documents right; a two-line display headline
"Any motion, frozen and checked." at about 64px with "frozen" in magenta; the prompt bar directly under it (about
900px wide, graphite, the yellow Run simulation button inside it on the right); attachments, documents and the
clarify toggle as quiet controls under the bar; below the fold line, demo cards whose thumbnails are real strobe
trails drawn from each demo's playback data. Job page: the 3D viewer leads, the build diagram above it while
building.

FORM: Strobe Study, Edgerton stroboscopic photography, IMPECCABLE’S PICK (my top-ranked grounded candidate, position
1 of 7); seed:2bab13378c5cae78. Signature move: the strobe trail. Demo and library thumbnails are multiple exposures
computed from real playback; the viewer gets a Strobe toggle that draws each moving object's past positions as
fading gel-coloured ghosts; each finished build stage fires a gel flash, so the diagram gets more colourful as the
build nears ready.
